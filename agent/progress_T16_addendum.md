# T16 addendum — multi-view depth Bayesian fusion at ERP overlap (2026-05-20)

- **Method**: per-ERP-pixel inverse-variance fusion with `w_i = sigmoid(conf_logit_i)` from Pi3, emitting fused depth + fused RGB + combined-precision field; nearest-pixel splat alongside a naive global z-buffer baseline. Code: `code/waymo2panorama/pipeline/depth_bayesian_fusion.py`, `scripts/phase3/run_bayesian_fusion.py`. Ran on anchors 60 + 90 (CPU, ~5 s each).
- **Result**: Bayesian fusion changes depth at overlap by mean **2.04 m on anchor 60** and **0.27 m on anchor 90** (RMSE 5.1 m / 1.1 m), so the depth product is materially cleaner — but only **1.8 - 2.3 % of ERP pixels are multi-cam**, so the visible RGB "double image" L3 ghost is **not noticeably reduced**. Headline goal (L3 ≥ +1 dB) not demonstrated; L3 RGB ghost is dominated by single-cam Pi3 mis-splat, not by cross-cam depth disagreement.
- **Recommendation**: integrate fused-depth as the default L3 depth-export path (strict upgrade for downstream 3D consumers), but frame in the paper as "cleaner 3D deliverable", NOT as a fix for the L3 RGB cycle-PSNR deficit. Full report: `notes/bayesian_fusion_report.md`. Outputs: `outputs/phase3/bayesian_fusion/anchor_{060,090}/{erp_*.{npy,png}, *_naive_vs_bayesian.png, coverage.png, summary.json}`.
